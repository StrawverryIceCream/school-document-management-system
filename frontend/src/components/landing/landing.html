Requester Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about requesting, clearances, and window claim policies.

What do I need to bring when claiming hard-copy credentials?
Always present your Valid Student ID (or government-issued photo ID) and display your digital claim pass barcode or QR code on your mobile device. If sending an authorized representative, generate an Authorized Delegation Slip in your portal and have them present a signed authorization letter plus photocopies of both IDs.
How long do documents stay available at the window before re-routing?
Official hard-copy records are held at your designated College Window for 15 calendar days following clearance notification. Unclaimed documents are automatically re-routed to the Registrar Vault for secure archiving.
Can faculty and teaching staff request credentials on the same portal?
Yes. Students and Teachers share a unified requester portal. Once logged in with your institutional credentials, the document catalog dynamically displays faculty-specific records (such as Certificates of Employment and Service Records) alongside fee waiver protocols.
What payment methods are supported for cashier settlement?
ARCH supports integrated automated payment gateways via GCash, Maya, Landbank Link.Biz, as well as on-campus cashier counter settlements using official receipt verification.
