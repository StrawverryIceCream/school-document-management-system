### 1. TUP Manila Main Campus Hierarchy (From Official Directory)

Unlike TUP Cavite (which uses a Campus Director and Assistant Directors), **TUP Manila** is governed by the **Board of Regents** and the **Office of the University President (OUP)**, supported by **four Vice Presidential (VP) Sectors** and **six Academic Colleges**:
